\section*{Common settings}
\textbf{Repo recipe} (all training before 2026-11-03): HRNet-W48 $384\times288$ FiDIP
(\texttt{w48\_384x288\_adam\_lr1e-3\_infant.yaml}), COCO-pose init, first 109 tensors frozen,
Adam LR $10^{-4}$, batch 20, 20 epochs, LR steps [10, 15], 4 workers, \texttt{gpu-short}, V100-SXM2 32\,GB,
4 CPU / 32\,GB. Training data \texttt{data/syrip} (200 real + 1000 synthetic); its 1000 synthetic labels were
later found to be wrong (2026-11-03). Test100 = \texttt{validate\_infant} (100 real images, correct
\texttt{test11.jpg} unless noted). Four Test100 images are byte-identical to training images (authors' split;
affects every Test100 number, including the paper's). Failed and cancelled jobs are not listed.
